# The story (A-01)

## Rory Sykes, 32, Malibu, Palisades Fire, January 8, 2025

Rory Sykes was 32, born blind, and had cerebral palsy that made it hard for him to walk. He lived in a small cottage on his family's land in the hills above Malibu. When the Palisades Fire reached the property, his mother, Shelley, couldn't lift him because her arm was broken, and when she called 911 the call wouldn't go through. So she drove to the nearest fire station, about a quarter mile away, to ask for help. When she got back with firefighters, Rory's cottage had burned to the ground and he was gone.

*Sources: F-01, F-02, F-03 in [`sources.md`](sources.md).*

### 20-second version for the stage (Aarya, 0:00–0:20)

> "Rory Sykes was 32. He was blind and couldn't walk well. When the Palisades Fire came, his mom had a broken arm and couldn't lift him. 911 didn't go through. She drove to the fire station for help, and when she got back, his home was gone. He didn't need a miracle. He needed two people and a phone call that connected."

### Rules for using this story

- **No photo of Rory** on slides or Devpost. His mother posted photos publicly, but we don't have her permission. Use text only.
- Say "his mother says" for anything that comes from her interviews (911 not working, no water at the station). These are her account, not an official finding.
- Don't say he "refused to leave". Some outlets reported he told his mother to save herself. It's true but it isn't our point, and it can sound like blame.
- Don't name the water district. Her criticism of it is her claim and it isn't our story.

## Backup story (if the team wants a non-LA one): Ernie Foss, 63, Paradise, Camp Fire, 2018

Ernie Foss was a musician who moved to Paradise because the Bay Area got too expensive. He had advanced lymphedema, couldn't walk, and used oxygen. A neighbor pounded on his door to warn him, but there were only minutes. He was found outside his home on Edgewood Lane with his dog, Bernice. *Sources: F-09, F-10.*

He maps onto our personas: can't walk, on oxygen, has a pet. If you use him, say "his family told reporters" for the details.
